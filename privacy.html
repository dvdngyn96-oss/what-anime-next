Privacy

There are no accounts, no ads, and no trackers. Nothing you do here is tied to your name, because the site never learns it.

What stays in your browser

Six things are saved on your own device and nowhere else:

  • Your watched list — shows you have marked as seen
  • A random id — so your ratings are not counted twice
  • Your own ratings — so the buttons remember what you picked
  • Your format filter — TV, ONA, OVA
  • Your year filter — whether you asked for 2010 or later
  • Anything looked up live — shows that were not already in the catalogue

None of it is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it. Clearing your browsing data for this site deletes all of it.

What is sent, only if you ask

Two things leave your device, and only when you press a button.

A rating you give a show. The show's id, whether you would recommend it, and your random id. That is the whole record.

Scores from a MyAnimeList import, if you opt in. Your export is read on your own machine. If you then choose to share, only the show ids and your scores are sent — never the file, never your username, never your plan-to-watch, and nothing that is not in this site's catalogue. You are shown the exact number before you decide, and you are asked every time rather than having a yes remembered.

You can take them back. Remove my ratings on the home page deletes them. That works for as long as this browser keeps its random id — if you have cleared your browsing data since, the ratings genuinely cannot be traced back to you any more, by me or by anyone else. That is what being properly anonymous costs, and it seemed more honest to say so than to imply otherwise.

What I never have

No name, no email address, no password, no account, no payment details, and no way to work out who you are from a rating.

Other companies your browser talks to

Loading a page here makes your browser fetch things from a few other places, which means those companies can see your IP address — the same as on almost any website:

  • MyAnimeList and AniList — poster images, cover art, and a show's synopsis and streaming listings
  • YouTube or Dailymotion — only if you press play on a trailer. YouTube embeds use its no-cookie mode
  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and provides visitor counts. The analytics are cookieless: no cookies, no fingerprinting, and nothing that follows you to other sites

Clearing everything

The home page has Clear for your watched list and Remove my ratings for anything you have shared. Beyond that, clearing site data for this site in your browser removes every trace on your end.
